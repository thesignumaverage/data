# Signum Average: data

The datasets behind Signum Average's articles and charts (website.thesignumaverage.workers.dev). There is one folder per article, named by publication date and title. Each folder holds the article's CSV files and a README that explains every file.

| Article folder | Datasets |
|---|---|
| [`2026-09-29_what-sanctions-cost-iranians/`](2026-09-29_what-sanctions-cost-iranians/) | Iran's enriched-uranium stockpile, with a source for every point; Iran vs "synthetic Iran": GDP per person gap; Inflation, Iran vs peer oil producers; Oil revenue, sanctions and household consumption: 60 model estimates; Household consumption in Iran: projections for 2026 and 2027 under three oil scenarios; Iran's economy in 2026: reported readings, with a source for every figure |
| [`2026-10-02_undiscovered-wine/`](2026-10-02_undiscovered-wine/) | Farmland suited to vineyards, and the vines grown on it, by country; Farmland suited to vineyards, by province; Wine market share and sales forgone by countries that plant little of their vineyard land; World wine production and consumption; Wine-grape area by country, 2000-2023; Vineyard-suitability model: continent hold-out test; Vineyard-suitability model: scores of known wine regions and control places; Wine regions: consumer ratings with the climate and soil of their vineyards; Vineyard-suitability model: 15 versions, one choice changed in each; Vineyard-suitability model: inside each country |
| [`2026-10-05_america-in-2075/`](2026-10-05_america-in-2075/) | Share of Americans counted as white to 2075, with ranges; Chance that each US state's white share falls below half; Population by group for every US state, projected to 2075 (scenarios); Year each US state's white share falls below half; How well the projection method would have called 2015 and 2020; Who moves to each US state from abroad; Who moves to the United States from abroad, by year; Children per white woman needed to hold each state's white population steady; The white share of Americans with and without immigration, to 2075; Births, deaths and net migration from abroad by group, United States; The projection method tested on 1990-2020; Test 1 of the ranges: an odds model fitted to the 1990s; Test 2 of the ranges: the published ranges against the method's past errors; The harsh reading: US white share to 2075 with wider ranges |
